01 Relational DBMS & Real-Time

Emergency 999

Real-Time National Emergency Incident Dispatch System

A mission-critical emergency dispatch and coordination platform enabling citizens across Bangladesh to report live distress incidents on an interactive map. Features automated unit dispatch algorithms, geospatial routing, and pessimistic concurrency management for high-load operational stability.

  • Engineered an 8-table relational schema with role-based access control (Citizen, Operator, Unit Commander, Admin).
  • Implemented Haversine-formula nearest-responder routing and 5 MySQL triggers to eliminate dispatch race conditions.
  • Built real-time incident geo-mapping using Leaflet.js and responsive asynchronous dispatch updating.

02 Web3 & Social Impact

RippleChain

Blockchain Land Registry & Compensation for Climate Refugees

A decentralized platform designed to protect property rights for climate-displaced citizens in river-erosion zones across Bangladesh. Provides tamper-proof deed recordation, cryptographic community validation, and direct automated compensation mechanisms.

  • Developed immutable land title registry contracts ensuring non-repudiation of historical property claims.
  • Constructed transparent multi-party validator voting for displacement verification and relief fund distribution.
